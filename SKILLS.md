# SKILLS — index

Three focused skill docs. Pick the one matching the role you're playing right now.

| Doc | When to read it |
|---|---|
| [SKILLS-wallet.md](SKILLS-wallet.md) | You want to **hold deposits.** Get a wallet, open deposits, receive, send, verify your balance, recover. |
| [SKILLS-operator.md](SKILLS-operator.md) | You want to **run a node.** Bring-up, configuration, the LDK sidecar, relays, cosign duties, dispute response, monitoring. |
| [SKILLS-protocol.md](SKILLS-protocol.md) | You want to **understand the mechanics** or investigate something weird. Wire format, hash chain, signatures, fraud proofs, the lottery, the explorer, forensic tools. |

Specifications are in the [DEPs](DEP-01.md). Operations runbooks are in [OPERATIONS.md](deposits-tools/OPERATIONS.md) (regtest) and [MAINNET_DEPLOYMENT.md](MAINNET_DEPLOYMENT.md).
