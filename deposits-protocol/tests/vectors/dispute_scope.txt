# DEP-03 §"The dispute": a disputed ledger's updates reduced to its current dispute.
# Q = the operator's latest QuorumBegin at or below the highest last_valid_sequence any
# DisputeEnter names. A disputant counts only through its latest DisputeEnter (by sequence) whose
# last_valid_sequence >= Q, and only its updates past that fork point; the operator's are kept.
#
#   u <who> <seq> open|qb|enter <lvs>|other     (who: o = the operator, a..z = disputants)
#   expect <who>:<seq> ...                       (the kept updates, in input order)
#   end
#
# 1. The soak case (2026-11-02): a's old dispute (on a vault since rotated) and its arm drop;
#    its new DisputeEnter and everything after it stay.
case stale-enter-drops
u o 0 open
u o 5 qb
u a 9 enter 8
u a 10 other
u o 20 qb
u o 30 other
u a 31 enter 30
u b 31 enter 30
u b 32 other
u a 32 other
expect o:0 o:5 o:20 o:30 a:31 b:31 b:32 a:32
end
# 2. A disputant with only a stale DisputeEnter drops out entirely.
case only-stale-drops-out
u o 0 open
u o 5 qb
u c 7 enter 6
u c 8 other
u o 20 qb
u b 21 enter 20
expect o:0 o:5 o:20 b:21
end
# 3. Two DisputeEnters at or above Q: the latest by sequence is the fork point.
case latest-enter-counts
u o 0 open
u o 5 qb
u a 11 enter 10
u a 12 other
u a 13 enter 12
u a 14 other
expect o:0 o:5 a:13 a:14
end
# 4. No QuorumBegin below the dispute (Q = 0): every DisputeEnter is in scope.
case no-quorum-begin
u o 0 open
u a 3 enter 2
u b 5 enter 4
u b 6 other
expect o:0 a:3 b:5 b:6
end
# 5. No dispute: unchanged.
case no-dispute
u o 0 open
u o 5 qb
u o 6 other
expect o:0 o:5 o:6
end
