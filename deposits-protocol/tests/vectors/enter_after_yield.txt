# DEP-06 "A yield binds only its author" (ruled 2026-11-24, "latest counts"): no other member
# reads a yield, and a disputant's later DisputeEnter supersedes its earlier fork for the scope
# (DEP-03 "The dispute": voters, accused set, arms) whatever that fork's yield says, or whether it
# yielded at all.  The scope is a function of the SET of updates, never of their order: a
# disputant's entries at or above Q in the canonical order (sequence; at one sequence a punitive
# entry before a quorum_expired one; then content hash ascending), the first counted and each
# later one at a higher sequence replacing it, except over a fork carrying its author's
# DisputeAcquire, which nothing supersedes; and the disputant takes part through that entry and
# the updates on its fork's chain only.  Superseding never ends a pledge (DEP-03 "When a pledge is
# live"): a pledge on a superseded fork stays held until its own valid yield or a binding claim of
# its round or a later one.
#
# deposits-rust: deposits-core/tests/enter_after_yield_vector.rs (rotation_order::dispute_scope,
# the scope cases) and deposits-node node::dispute (forks_hold, the pledge cases).  cl-deposits:
# inspect/lottery-test.lisp (dispute-scope; superseded-entry / superseded-pledges-held and
# fork-pledges-held for the pledge cases) and inspect/node-test.lisp (its ingest, in every arrival
# order).  Both run each scope case in every order of its updates (all permutations up to six
# updates; else the input order, its reverse and 300 shuffles) and require the same scope.
#
# Scope cases:
#   case <name> ... end
#   u <who> <seq> <kind> [args] [h=<height>] [as=<tag>] [on=<tag>]
#       who: o = the ledger's operator, a..z = disputants; tag <who>:<seq>, or as=<tag>.
#       kinds: open | other | qb | enter <lvs> [punitive] [cont] | arm [<round>] | acquire
#              | yield [lost_to=<name>] [excluded_by=<name>] [reestablished=<tag>] [superseded_by=<tag>]
#   expect <tag> ...         dispute_scope's kept updates, in input order
# Building them: each who's key the compressed public key of the secret key 32 x <its character
# code>; ledger_id 32 x 0x09, block_hash 32 x 0x01, signature 64 x 0, no cosignatures, block_height
# h (default 0); content_hash the update's DEP-02 hash and its chain hash SHA256(content_hash ||
# signature), as for any update.  previous_hash: an operator update's, the chain hash of the
# operator's update before it (32 x 0 for the first); an enter's, the chain hash of the operator's
# update at its last_valid_sequence, or with "cont" of its fork's latest update (a continuation
# onto the fork's tip); any other disputant update's, the chain hash of the latest update on its
# fork (its latest DisputeEnter's, or on=<tag>'s); a chain hash of a tag no update carries is
# SHA256(SHA256("enter_after_yield:" || tag) || 64 x 0).  A punitive enter's reason "equivocation", any
# other "quorum_expired"; a yield's TLVs SHA256("tx:" || name) for lost_to / excluded_by, and for
# reestablished / superseded_by the content hash of the update tagged so (SHA256("enter_after_yield:"
# || tag) when there is none); an arm: armed_block 0, commitment 20 x 0, target "", rearm_round
# <round> when > 1; an acquire: new_custodian the key of "m", claim_txid SHA256("tx:claim"),
# new_reserves_address "".
#
# Pledge cases, one disputant's (our) forks of one ledger:
#   pledge <name> ... end
#   fork <n> enter | arm <coin> [<round>] | yield seen | yield void | acquire binds | acquire pending
#                            fork n's own dispute operations, oldest first; a void yield names a
#                            transaction not confirmed (DEP-06 "An invalid yield is void")
#   supersede <n>            our later DisputeEnter (the next fork) supersedes fork n
#   later-seen <n>           after that, fork n's void yield's transaction confirms (now valid)
#   others <round>           another participant's binding claim closes this round (every fork)
#   held <coin> ... | held -   the coins still held, sorted by name
# A coin is the outpoint SHA256("tx:" || coin):0.

# 1. A lost_to yield whose claim is not confirmed (pending): the new entry is taken anyway.
case lost-to-pending-taken
u o 0 open
u o 5 qb
u a 6 enter 5
u a 7 arm
u a 8 yield lost_to=C h=100
u o 10 other
u a 11 enter 10
expect o:0 o:5 o:10 a:11
end
# 2. An excluded_by yield, pending or forged: taken; b, who never yielded, is unaffected.
case excluded-by-taken
u o 0 open
u o 5 qb
u a 6 enter 5
u b 6 enter 5
u a 7 arm
u a 8 yield excluded_by=L h=100
u o 10 other
u a 11 enter 10
u b 11 other
expect o:0 o:5 b:6 o:10 a:11 b:11
end

# P1. An armed fork superseded by our re-entry keeps its pledge; the new fork's is held too.
pledge armed-superseded-held
fork 1 enter
fork 1 arm A
supersede 1
fork 2 enter
fork 2 arm B
held A B
end
# P2. Superseded after a void yield (its claim not confirmed): still held.
pledge void-yield-superseded-held
fork 1 enter
fork 1 arm A
fork 1 yield void
supersede 1
fork 2 enter
held A
end
# P3. ...and released once that yield's claim confirms (its own valid yield).
pledge void-yield-later-valid-releases
fork 1 enter
fork 1 arm A
fork 1 yield void
supersede 1
fork 2 enter
later-seen 1
held -
end

end
# P5. Superseded, then another participant's binding claim of its round: released; the new

# P6. A superseded fork's re-arm (round 2) pledge outlives a claim that closes round 1 only.
